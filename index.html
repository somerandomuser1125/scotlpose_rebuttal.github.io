3. Does energy model bring better structure?

We summarize here how the learned energy from the loss-net relates to structural plausibility:

  • Following the loss-net gradient (GBI) improves poses: A few gradient steps on the loss-net energy consistently reduce both P-MPJPE and structural errors (LSE, BSLE, LLE), so the energy gives meaningful guidance, not random regularization.
  • Effect is not just “lower MPJPE”: Even when baseline and SCoTL-Pose have matched P-MPJPE, SCoTL-Pose still has lower LSE/BSLE/LLE, meaning the loss-net has learned preferences for more structurally plausible poses beyond MPJPE.
  • Energy is aligned with structural metrics: Within fixed P-MPJPE bins, the graph-based loss-net energy is positively correlated with LSE/BSLE/LLE; for LSE, Kendall τ≈0.24–0.26 (~62–63% correct ordering), showing strong sensitivity to symmetry and limb-length consistency.
  • Graph-based > MLP loss-net: Compared to an MLP, the graph-based loss-net has consistently higher correlation (~+0.1 τ) with all structural metrics, indicating that explicitly encoding the skeleton graph makes the energy much better at ranking structural plausibility.

4. Pose-net and Loss-net diagram

The figure below illustrates the overall architecture of the pose-net and the graph-based loss-net.

Given input 2D keypoints, the pose-net lifts them to 3D keypoints. The resulting 3D keypoints are then concatenated with the input 2D keypoints and a joint embedding to form joint-level node features.

These node features are fed into a graph-based loss-net, where attention is biased using a skeleton-derived adjacency that encodes the kinematic structure. A CLS token aggregates global information and the loss-net outputs a single energy scalar for the pose, which acts as a learned structure-preserving loss.

During training, the pose-net and loss-net are updated in an alternating manner, so that the loss-net learns to assign lower energy to structurally plausible poses and higher energy to implausible ones, while the pose-net learns to minimize this energy together with the supervised error.
